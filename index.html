About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Industrial Scrap Metal Trading

LCZ Golden Metal Trade is a trusted Malaysian industrial scrap metal sourcing and trading company connecting suppliers and buyers through professional brokerage, recycling solutions, and supply chain coordination.

We are committed to integrity, transparency, professionalism, and sustainable recycling practices. Our team delivers competitive sourcing solutions while building lasting partnerships across Southeast Asia and beyond.
